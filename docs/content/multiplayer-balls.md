# Multiplayer balls

Want a few more ways to help your table? Before starting, the host can enable **Mod settings → Multiplayer balls**.

These eight balls then appear occasionally in the shop. They never replace your starting rack. You can inspect all eight in the **Together** collection even when the setting is off.

## Meet the set

| Ball | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Relay** | Touch it on one shot, then let a teammate pot it later for +50% ball value. At a solo table or in Score PvP, any later shot qualifies. |
| **Called Shot** | The previous shooter calls a pocket. Pot the highlighted ball there on the next shot for +100% ball value. |
| **Patience** | Survive a shot that hits an object ball to store +25% ball value. Hold up to three charges, then pot it to collect. |
| **Bounty** | In Score PvP, the earliest successful shot earns 25 match points. Tied tables each get the award. In co-op or Race, pot it within the match’s first three shots for 10 run points. |
| **Bankroll** | Bounce it off a cushion and pot it on the same shot for two shared coins. |
| **Lifeline** | Pot it to recover one shared health, up to your normal maximum. |
| **Encore** | Pot it after an ordinary ball to bring the most recently potted eligible ball back once movement settles. You’ll need another shot to play it again. |
| **Domino** | Pot it to give the next ordinary ball pocketed on that shot +100% ball value. |

## A few rules

Bankroll, Lifeline, Encore, and Domino work once per table per round. There’s one Bounty award per match. Relay, Called Shot, and Patience pay once per ball per round.

Passing doesn’t charge abilities or advance the Bounty deadline. Bonuses use ball value before pocket multipliers.

To change a Called Shot, click another pocket. With several Called Shot balls, select the ball first. The matching highlights show the call.
