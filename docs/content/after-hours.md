# After the final shot

**Coming in a future update.** This ending screen is not in the latest download.

## Look back at your build

When your table finishes, the **After-hours rack** lets you browse its final build while the other tables keep playing.

Choose a finished table to see its recorded triangle, reserve balls, and snacks, along with its players, final score, shots, round, and cues. Extra balls and negative cubes appear below. Hover an item for its name, level, and details.

The number on each ball is its saved base points plus temporary points. It isn’t the total that ball earned during the run.

Unfinished tables show their current status. Choose **Lobby & live tables** to check the standings or watch someone play. **Final racks** brings you back to the review.

## Talk through the rack

Drag a ball into an empty slot, or drop it onto another ball to swap their places. Everyone viewing that table sees the movement and the new arrangement. One person can hold a ball in each table’s rack at a time.

Press **Escape** to cancel a move. After putting the ball down, choose **Reset arrangement** to restore the recorded layout for everyone.

These moves are for discussing the build. They don’t change the saved build, ball values, or match scores.

## See how everyone finished

Once all tables finish, a brief chalk animation marks the leading table. Tied scores share the result. Race follows finishing order.

## Move on when you’re ready

**Continue** becomes available when all tables have finished and their final racks are ready to view. It opens your result card, or returns you to the lobby after a Score PvP match.

If a disconnected table’s build is unavailable, it’s marked that way and won’t stop everyone leaving. Multiplayer endless runs aren’t supported.
