# Cue Workshop

Visit **Rook, to the right of the snack bar**, to browse fifteen cues. Choose a cue to see its effect, try a finish, or buy it.

Purchases use your table’s shared money, but the cue belongs to you. Buying equips it. You can switch between owned cues for free while shopping. Cues last for the run; everyone starts the next run with House.

## Pick your cue

| Cue | Price | Effect |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| House | Free | Standard power and scoring. |
| Finesse | 2€ | Gentler light shots; firmer heavy shots. |
| Firm | 2€ | Firmer light shots; gentler heavy shots. |
| Clean | 3€ | Pot without that ball touching a rail: +3%, up to +1 point. |
| Opener | 3€ | Pot on the table’s first shot of the round: +6%, up to +1.5. |
| Silk | 4€ | Pot on a light shot: +4%, up to +1.5. |
| Thunder | 4€ | Pot on a heavy shot: +4%, up to +1.5. |
| Comeback | 4€ | Pot after your previous shot this round had no scoring pots: +6%, up to +1.5. |
| Corner | 5€ | Pot into a corner pocket: +3%, up to +1.5. |
| Sidewinder | 5€ | Pot into a middle pocket: +5%, up to +1.5. |
| Carom | 5€ | Pot a ball after it touches two different object balls: +10%, up to +2.5. |
| Double Rail | 6€ | Pot a ball after two rail contacts: +9%, up to +2.5. |
| Bankshot | 7€ | Pot a ball after a rail contact: +6%, up to +2. |
| Relay | 7€ | Pot after a teammate made a scoring pot on the previous shot: +5%, up to +2. |
| Closer | 8€ | Pot after starting the shot with three or fewer ordinary balls: +5%, up to +2. |

Each shot can earn the listed bonus once, on the first pot that meets the cue’s condition. Bonuses use the ball’s value before pocket multipliers. You can earn up to **four bonus points per player per round** across all cues. Switching cues doesn’t reset that limit.

Closer counts base-game balls. The cue ball and balls with Together or expansion effects, including mixes, don’t count.

## Choose a finish

Ten finishes are free: Native, Emerald, Coral, Gold, Violet, Ice, Rose, Chalk, Midnight, and Amber. Your finish carries into future runs and changes appearance only.

Before a match, use **Mod settings → Your starting cue finish**. During a run, use the workshop.

## Keep it simple

The host can turn **Cue shop** off in the lobby before starting. Everyone uses House without perks; personal finishes remain available.
