# Shopping together

Your table shares one wallet and inventory. By default, everyone can buy, sell, mix, and arrange items between rounds.

After the payout, choose **Continue** to enter the shop. Drag balls and snacks as you would in the normal game. Teammate cursors help you see what everyone is working on.

## Ready when you are

Choose **Ready** when you’re done. The next round starts once every connected teammate is ready.

A purchase, rearrangement, or change of teammates clears readiness. Take one last look at the build, then ready up again.

If two players try to buy the same item, only one purchase can go through. If your purchase fails, you keep your money.

## Browse your own counter

You can visit the ball shop, snack bar, or mixer without moving anyone else’s view.

Want to follow along instead? Open **Mod settings → Follow host shop view**. Your view will follow the host between counters after you finish any active drag or purchase. Turn it off whenever you want to browse freely. Your choice is remembered.

## Let one person shop

Before a match, the host can turn **Shared shop access** off. Each table’s host then handles its shopping while teammates wait. This choice stays fixed for the match.

Some special rounds give shopping rights to one winner. That round’s rule takes priority.
